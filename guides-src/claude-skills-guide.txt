## What is a skill?

A skill is a set of instructions that teaches Claude how you work. Your brand voice. Your meeting format. Your coding style. Your formatting standards. Instead of re-explaining all of that every new chat, you write it down once — and Claude already knows. Every time.

*"They're not prompting harder. They're building systems."*

## How to set one up

# 1. Open Claude Desktop

Go to Customize → Skills → Browse.

# 2. Install the Skill Creator

Find "Skill Creator" in the browse menu and hit Install.

# 3. Open a new chat

The Skill Creator is now available inside any chat.

# 4. Build your skill

**Option A:** Describe the skill you want — Claude asks questions and builds it.

**Option B:** Walk through a workflow you already do, then tell Claude to save it as a skill.

# 5. Click Install and you're done

Your skill is live. Next time you need it, Claude calls it automatically.

## 8 skills to build first

Not sure where to start? These are the highest-leverage skills for most people.

- **Meeting Notes Summarizer.** Paste messy notes and get a clean summary in your exact format, every time.
- **Brand Voice Writer.** Claude writes in your tone without you pasting guidelines each time.
- **Email Drafter.** Describe what you need to say and Claude drafts it in your style.
- **Weekly Report Builder.** Drop in raw updates and Claude structures your weekly report automatically.
- **Reel Script Polish.** Paste a rough idea and Claude shapes it into a script in your voice.
- **Caption Writer.** Feed it your script and Claude writes the caption, CTA, and hashtags.
- **Research Summarizer.** Paste any article or doc and Claude extracts the key points your way.
- **Client Proposal Template.** Describe the project and Claude builds a proposal in your structure.

## When does Claude call your skill?

You don't have to think about it. Claude recognizes when a task matches a skill you've installed and calls it automatically. Here's what that looks like in practice:

| You type this | Claude does this |
| "Summarize my meeting notes" + paste notes | Calls your Meeting Notes skill and outputs in your exact format |
| "Write a caption for this reel" + paste script | Calls your Caption Writer skill, in your voice with your CTA structure |
| "Draft an email to my client about the delay" | Calls your Email Drafter skill, so it sounds like you and not a template |
| "Turn this into a report" | Calls your Report Builder skill with your headers and your structure |
