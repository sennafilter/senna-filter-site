## What's a Cowork plugin?

A bundle of connectors and skills that turns Claude into a specific kind of worker — sales, legal, marketing, and more. Install from Cowork.

Claude has dozens of Cowork plugins sitting there right now, and most people never open one. Each one turns Claude into a specific kind of worker for your business — researching, drafting, tracking, flagging — instead of just answering when you ask. Here are five worth installing this week, what each one actually does, and who it's built for.

# 1. Marketing

~ Writes in your brand voice and tells you what's actually working.

- Drafts on-brand copy for email, social, and ads once it knows your voice
- Builds out campaign plans and content calendars
- Pulls performance data across channels so you're not guessing

**Best for:** solo marketers and small teams covering every channel at once.

# 2. Productivity

~ Catches what's slipping through the cracks before it becomes a fire.

- Scans your email, calendar, and chat threads for anything that needs action
- Tracks it all in one live dashboard instead of five different apps
- Flags what's overdue or about to be

**Best for:** anyone managing too many threads across too many tools.

# 3. Sales

~ Runs the parts of your pipeline you don't have time to babysit.

- Researches a prospect before you ever send the first message
- Drafts the cold email and the follow-up sequence
- Flags deals in your pipeline that are going quiet
- Pulls competitor intel so you know what you're up against

**Best for:** founders and reps running their own pipeline solo.

# 4. Legal

~ A first pass on any contract before it costs you something.

- Reads any contract or NDA you drop in
- Flags risky clauses in red, watch-outs in yellow, safe language in green
- Drafts redline language you can send back

**Best for:** a fast first read — not a replacement for an actual lawyer on anything that matters.

> **Not legal advice.** Have counsel review anything with real money or risk attached.

# 5. Small Business

~ Fifteen prebuilt skills standing in for a handful of hires.

- Invoicing and month-end close
- Tax prep support
- Full marketing campaigns
- The back-office work that usually gets pushed to Sunday night

**Best for:** the business owner doing five jobs at once. This is the one to install first.

## How to install

Open Cowork, search the plugin by name, and install it. It'll ask you to connect a few tools — email, calendar, whatever it needs to actually do the job. Takes a few minutes.

You don't need to build any of these workflows from scratch. They already exist. You just have to open them.
