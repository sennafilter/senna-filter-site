## What is a prompt prefix?

A prompt prefix is a word or short phrase you type at the beginning of any message to Claude. It acts like an instruction mode — it tells Claude how to approach your question before it even reads it.

Think of it like adjusting a dial. Same question, completely different output depending on the prefix you use.

Most prompts people use are just questions. Prompt prefixes are a layer on top of that. They tell Claude what thinking style to use, not just what to answer.

## The four prefixes that actually matter

# 1. ELI5

~ Explain like I'm five

Tells Claude to strip out jargon and rebuild the explanation from the ground up — simple analogies, plain language, no assumed knowledge. Great for anything technical, legal, or just unfamiliar.

**Example:** ELI5 how does compound interest work?

**What Claude does:** Claude skips the financial formulas and explains it like a snowball rolling downhill — your money earns money, and then that money earns money. Simple enough to actually stick.

# 2. Steel man then kill it

~ Adversarial two-step review

Claude first argues the strongest possible version of your idea, then systematically tears it apart. Built-in devil's advocate — without having to ask twice.

**Example:** Steel man then kill it: I should move to a new city for a fresh start.

**What Claude does:** Claude makes the strongest case for why it could be the best decision of your life — then dismantles it, surfacing the things you haven't let yourself think about yet.

# 3. Red team this

~ Every critic in the room, all at once

Claude attacks your plan or decision from every angle a skeptic would. Like having a room full of people who want to find the flaw — before it finds you.

**Example:** Red team this: my plan to go back to school for a career change.

**What Claude does:** Claude surfaces the hardest objections — cost, timeline, opportunity cost, whether the degree actually leads to the job — in thirty seconds instead of two years of regret.

# 4. Break this down from first principles

~ No recycled internet answers

Tells Claude to stop pattern-matching on common answers and rebuild the logic from scratch. Instead of summarizing what everyone else says, it reasons through it from fundamental truths.

**Example:** Break this down from first principles: why is sleep so important?

**What Claude does:** Instead of listing the usual bullet points, Claude reasons through what your brain and body are actually doing — memory consolidation, cellular repair, hormone regulation — from the ground up.

## How to use them

These work with any topic. Just type the prefix, then your actual question or content right after it.

The format:

```
[prefix] + [your normal question or content]
```

Examples:

- Red team this: my plan to negotiate a raise at work.
- ELI5 what is the difference between a Roth IRA and a traditional IRA?
- Break this down from first principles: why do habits take so long to form?

You can also stack them. "Steel man then kill it" already combines two instructions — you can do the same with others.
