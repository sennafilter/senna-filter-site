About me

You might know me from @sennafilter.

I make content about AI for people who don't have "tech" anywhere in their title. What it actually is, what it's good and bad at, and how to use it at work and in real life.

By day I do AI strategy, figuring out where AI actually makes sense inside a business and where it doesn't. Then I come here and make it useful for everyone else.

My degree is actually in biochemistry. I taught myself data analytics from scratch, got named a Microsoft MVP three times over, and then broke into AI. None of it the expected way. So I know what it's like to stare at something that sounds way over your head and feel completely out of your depth, and figure it out anyway.

That's my whole job here. I take AI and tech and explain it like a real person, so it actually clicks.
