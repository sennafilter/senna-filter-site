Workshops

Two ways to run a workshop.

Start with the foundations or go deeper and learn to build. Every workshop is tailored to your team, your tools, and the work you actually do.

Where's your team right now?

One focused day

The AI Foundations Workshop

1 day

A practical, hands-on introduction to using AI at work. Your team learns which tools matter, how to use them effectively, and where AI can actually make a difference in their day-to-day work.

This isn't a day of slides and AI theory. We work through real examples, real workflows, and real tasks your team can start using AI for immediately.

  • Understand the AI tools worth knowing
  • Learn how to get consistently better results
  • Apply AI to real work your team does every day
  • Find opportunities to save time and work smarter
  • Build practical AI habits your team can keep using
Go deeper

The AI Builder Intensive

2–5 days

This is where your team goes from using AI to building with it.

Over several hands-on days, we identify high-value opportunities inside your team's actual workflows and turn them into working AI tools, assistants, and automations. No technical background required.

By the end, your team hasn't just learned what's possible — they've built things they can actually use.

  • Build custom AI assistants for specific roles and workflows
  • Create AI-powered tools and internal sites without traditional coding
  • Automate repetitive, time-consuming work
  • Learn how to connect AI tools into more powerful workflows
  • Leave with working solutions and the skills to keep building
