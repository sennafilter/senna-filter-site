Workshops

Where's your team right now?

One focused day

The Fluency Workshop

1 day · whole team

Everyone's heard of AI. Almost no one on a team actually uses it well. In one focused day, your whole team goes from dabbling to working with AI like they mean it — on the real work they do every day.

  • The handful of tools actually worth their time
  • How to get sharp results, not generic ones
  • Hands-on with your team's real work
  • Habits that stick long after we're done
Best for: getting a whole team using AI well, fast.
Go deeper

The Builder Intensive

2–5 days · hands-on

A multi-day workshop that goes past the basics. Your team learns to actually build with AI — custom assistants, simple tools and sites, and automations for the work they do every day. They build it, so they own it. Length scales with team size and how far you want to go.

  • Build custom AI assistants and subagents
  • Vibe-code a working tool or site — no engineers needed
  • Automate the repetitive workflows your team runs
  • Everyone leaves able to build, not just use
Best for: teams ready to build their own tools, not just use AI.
