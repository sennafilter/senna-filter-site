Questions

Good questions, quick answers.

When does it open?

January 4th. Waitlist members get an email the moment the doors open.

Do I need any experience with AI?

No. The course starts from the very beginning and builds step by step. If you already use AI, you'll still pick up better habits and build things you haven't yet.

Do I need to know how to code?

No coding experience is required. In the building lessons, you'll learn to read what AI creates and ask it to explain, never to write code from scratch.

What tools do I need?

A computer, an internet connection, and free accounts for the tools you'll try. The lessons tell you when something needs a paid plan.

How long does it take, and is it live?

It's self-paced: about 80 short recorded lessons you can do on your own schedule.

Will it go out of date?

AI is changing fast, so I'll always keep the course updated.

How long do I get access?

Lifetime. Once you're in, it's yours for good.
