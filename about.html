About me

You might know me from @sennafilter.

I'm helping you navigate this whole AI thing — especially for people who don't have "tech" anywhere in their title. I help you understand what it actually is, what it's good at and bad at, and how you can use it in real life and at work.

My background is in AI strategy and transformation — figuring out where AI actually makes sense inside a business, and where it doesn't. I take that same work and make it useful for everyone else.

But my degree is actually in biochemistry. I taught myself data analytics from scratch, got named a Microsoft MVP three times over, and then broke into AI. None of it the expected way. So I know exactly what it's like to stare at something that sounds way over your head and feel completely overwhelmed and lost.

That's my whole job here: I take tech and AI and explain it like a real person, so it actually clicks.
